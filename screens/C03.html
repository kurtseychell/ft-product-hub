Story US-101

Smart Batching Logic

As a frequent user, I want my low-priority notifications to be batched and delivered twice daily so that I am not constantly interrupted during my deep-work hours.

fact_check Acceptance Criteria
  • Batch times must be configurable (e.g., 9 AM and 4 PM).
  • Any 'Critical' alert must bypass batching immediately.
  • Users can view the 'Batched' tray at any time to see pending alerts.
Story US-102

Activity-Aware Delivery

As a mobile user, I want the app to detect when I am actively using my device so that it can deliver non-critical alerts as subtle in-app banners instead of loud push notifications.
