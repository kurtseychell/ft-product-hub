PRD · Cash Out v1

Early settlement for single bets

Authored by R. Kim, K. Carabott · 18 April 2026 · Target release Q3 2026

§1 Overview

Cash Out is a punter-initiated early-settlement feature that lets a customer close a live bet for a dynamically-calculated amount before the final settlement event. v1 covers single bets only on football, tennis and basketball, across pre-match and in-play states.

§2 Problem & evidence

Our book is the only top-15 European operator without any form of cash-out. Q1 2026 churn analysis flagged this as the #1 cited missing feature:

  • 18% of exit-survey respondents mentioned "cash out" unprompted
  • 2,412 support-chat mentions in the last 90 days
  • Estimated €12M of NGR at risk from the single-bet segment alone

§3 Goals & non-goals

Goals — available on ≥ 85% of single bets by launch+30d; reduce single-bet churn by ≥ 12% YoY; maintain book margin 6.8–8.5%.

Non-goals — partial cash-out, accumulators & system bets, and auto cash-out rules are all explicitly deferred to phase 2.

§4 Odds model

Cash-out offer is computed as stake × (currentOdds / originalOdds) × (1 − margin). The dynamic margin lives in a 4–8% band, widened by liquidity, volatility, and current liability on the market. A 2-second refresh cadence keeps the offer in line with the live market; suspensions lock the offer for 800ms while the trading desk re-prices.

Trading guard-rail: if a single punter's cash-out would move liability by > €5k on a thin market, the request is queued for trader approval within 1.5s.

§5 Punter UX

A persistent "Cash Out" button appears under each active single bet in the Bet History drawer and on the Live Event page. The amount pulses green when rising and red when falling. Confirmation is a single tap with a 3-second undo toast.

§6 Risk & compliance

Cash-out offers respect self-exclusion and deposit limits — a self-excluded punter sees existing bets run to natural settlement and cannot initiate a cash-out. Every offer and acceptance is logged with a signed hash for the regulator audit trail (MGA §3.5, UKGC LCCP 9.1).

§7 Rollout

Shadow-mode in staging for 2 weeks (no punter exposure), 1% canary on Malta, ramp to 10% over 72h, full rollout gated on GGR-margin holding within 6.8–8.5%.

§8 Success metrics

See §8 of the E04 Metrics dashboard for live instrumentation. Primary north-star: single-bet churn reduction. Secondary: cash-out usage rate, 30-day return rate for cash-out users.
