Product Hub · Design Handoff

10 screens, one system.

This document collects every new and re-skinned screen into a single scrollable artifact. Three screens (C02, E04, Z01) were previously built against IBM Carbon; they are now rebuilt on the canonical Product Hub kit — indigo #3525CD, Inter + Manrope, dark-navy sidebar, rounded tokens, Material 3 color roles. The four Bug screens and three Admin v2 screens are net-new, composed entirely from existing primitives.

3 · Re-skins
From Carbon → Product Hub
C02 RICE Scoring, E04 Success Metrics, Z01 LLM Usage & Costs. Same information architecture, new visual language.
4 · Bug pipeline
Net-new workstream
Intake (form), Triage (queue), Workspace (3-panel AI), Analytics (trends). Mirrors B-series PRD flow.
3 · Admin v2
Platform operations
Users & Roles, Integrations, Audit Log. Extends Z01 admin shell with the full settings catalog.
