Product Hub

The canonical kit.

Every Product Hub screen — from Brief to Release — is built from the primitives on this page. It consolidates the patterns already present in 17 aligned screens (A01, A02, B01–B05, C01, C03, C04, D01–D04, E01–E03) into one reference, so three outliers (C02, E04, Z01) previously built against IBM Carbon can be re-skinned against a shared source of truth and three new workstreams (Bug pipeline, Admin v2) can be generated consistently.
